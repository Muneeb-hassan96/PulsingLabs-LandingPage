Refund & Cancellation

When you get your money back, clearly and up front.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

You pay for your test package in the app when you book. This page explains when you can change or cancel, and when you get your money back. The same rules are shown on the payment screen before you pay.

1. Changing or cancelling your booking

  • 24 hours or more before your visit: reschedule or cancel in the app, free. Cancelling gives a full refund.
  • In the last 24 hours: the visit can't be cancelled or moved in the app. Our lab calls you to arrange any change.
  • After your sample has been collected: the booking can't be cancelled, because testing has started.

2. Visit charges

A visit is missed if you are not home when the phlebotomist arrives, or if it has to move in the last 24 hours because of you (you ask to move it, or the lab can't reach you).

  • The first time: free. You pick a new time at no charge.
  • Any later time: visit charges of 20% of what you paid apply. Pay them in the app and pick a new time (what you already paid covers the rest), or cancel and get the other 80% back.

Example: on a Standard booking of PKR 9,999, the visit charges are PKR 2,000. If you cancel instead, you get PKR 7,999 back.

3. When you get a full refund

  • We or the lab cancel your visit.
  • Your payment went through but the booking could not be made.
  • You were charged twice for the same booking.

If your sample can't be tested because of a problem on our side or the lab's (for example, the sample is damaged or rejected), we collect a new sample at no cost.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with. We approve them and send you an email showing the amount; our payment provider then processes them, and how long the money takes to show depends on your bank or wallet.

5. Questions or a problem with a refund

Email contact@pulsinglabs.com with your booking number and we will sort it out.
