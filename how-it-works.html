How a home blood test works

You book and pay in the PulsingLabs app, a phlebotomist comes to your home in Karachi, and your results arrive in the app, explained for your age and sex.

The short answer

Choose Standard (PKR 9,999, 12 tests) or Premium (PKR 16,999, 23 tests), pick a time, and pay in the app. A phlebotomist from a licensed partner lab collects your sample at home. No fasting is needed, the packages are for all ages, and the price includes the home visit.

Step by step

  1. Choose a package. Standard is a first check-up: vitals, blood count, sugar, cholesterol, liver, kidney and calcium. Premium adds HbA1c, a full lipid panel, ferritin, ESR, thyroid (TSH), vitamin D and hepatitis B and C screening. See both packages.
  2. Say who it is for. Book for yourself or for a family member on your account. Each person keeps their own results.
  3. Add your address and phone. Drop a pin on the map and give a number the phlebotomist can call.
  4. Pick a day and time. The app only shows times when a home visit is free. A visit on the same day needs at least 6 hours' notice.
  5. Pay in the app. The booking is made once your payment succeeds, and you get an invoice by email and in the app.
  6. We arrange the lab. We assign a licensed partner lab to your visit and confirm it in the app. You get a reminder the day before.
  7. The visit. At your door the phlebotomist scans the visit code shown in your app (or you read out its six digits), so you know it is the right person. They take your pulse and blood pressure, then the blood sample.
  8. Your results. The lab tests your sample and signs off the results. In the app you see each value on a chart for your age and sex, with a plain explanation, and you can ask the AI assistant about them in English or Urdu.

No fasting is needed for any test in either package. Eat and drink as usual before your visit.

If you need to change your visit

  • 24 hours or more before: reschedule or cancel in the app, free. Cancelling gives a full refund.
  • In the last 24 hours: the app can't change it; our lab calls you to arrange any change.
  • Missed visits: if the visit has to move because of you in those 24 hours, or you are not home when we arrive, the first time is free. After that, visit charges of 20% of what you paid apply.

Your privacy

Your results are private to your account and never sold. If your employer runs a health programme, HR only sees whether you booked and completed your check-up, never your results.

Common questions

Do I need to fast before a home blood test?

No. Every test in both packages is non-fasting, so you can eat and drink as usual.

Can children and older parents be tested?

Yes. The packages are for all ages, and results are explained using ranges for each person's age and sex.

Can I book for my parents from my account?

Yes. Add them as family members, then book a home visit for them. Each relative gets their own Health ID and results.
